# Explore Karakuram — Asset & Content Audit

Source audited: https://www.explorekarakuram.com/  
Audit date: 2026-08-18

## What was collected

- 41 image references discovered on the rendered homepage.
- 13 stylesheets and 4 web fonts identified.
- 55 usable image, font, and stylesheet files downloaded successfully.
- Original downloads are preserved in `public/assets/source/` with the browser-generated manifest.
- Human-readable copies are organized in `public/assets/images/`, `public/assets/fonts/`, and `public/assets/stylesheets/`.

The old `mail.explorekarakuram.com` logo URL and one insecure Google Fonts stylesheet were unavailable. The original logo is therefore represented as a clean CSS mountain mark in the new build. A Google advertising tracking pixel was intentionally not treated as a brand asset.

## Design direction

The new experience keeps the original brand promise, destinations, contact information, and photography, while replacing the dated WordPress travel-template presentation with a premium editorial system:

- Deep alpine green, glacial blue, warm expedition gold
- Large serif storytelling headlines with compact utility typography
- Strong full-bleed photography and tactile cards
- Clear journey discovery and direct enquiry paths
- Mobile-first responsive layout and accessible navigation
